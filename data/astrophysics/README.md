# Astrophysics and cosmology catalogue

The collection contains 143 formulas from 22 public research papers and lecture-note sources, organised into 90 families across 15 topics. In the formula ledger, select **Astrophysics** to browse formulas, expand families, or search by topic, formula name or source.

- [formulas.tsv](formulas.tsv) records each formula, its family and role, its output, and any assumptions or approximations. Related expressions and special cases share a family ID.
- [sources.json](sources.json) maps source IDs to citations and PDF URLs. The table gives the one-based PDF page and equation or section locator for each formula.
- [astrophysics.json](../catalog/astrophysics.json) is the generated data used by the ledger. It includes the source links, expression trees and operator counts.

See the [reproduction guide](../../docs/reproduction.md#build-the-catalogs) to regenerate the JSON from the curated table.
